# Isolate multiple Design documents

The one-domain-per-document restriction is superseded for the target block
workflow by [ADR-0030](0030-reference-design-domains-per-operation-block.md).
The paragraph below retains the original decision for historical context.

The workspace will own multiple Design documents but render and operate on one Active design at a time. Each document owns its Design domain, field-object scene, parameters, implicit results, derived meshes, and caches; field objects are copied rather than shared across documents. A Design creation source is either an imported STL or an Analytic design domain, whose evaluator remains authoritative rather than being converted to STL. Removing a design creates an Archived design, which cannot participate in rendering or commands until restored; permanent removal is explicit. Deleting the Active design selects another active document when available, otherwise leaves an Empty Design workspace. A new application starts with this empty state; example geometry requires an explicit create command. The workspace persists documents and archived state across restart, while disposable sampled-field and renderer caches are rebuilt instead of serialized. Imported design-domain and custom unit-cell STL sources are copied into managed workspace assets and referenced there, rather than depending on their original external paths. Generated and exported STL results are also retained as managed assets with provenance, but sampled implicit fields and renderer resources remain disposable. Interactive edits mark the workspace dirty; persistence occurs only through explicit save, close confirmation, or workspace-switch confirmation. A background task is bound to the document identifier and Design revision captured at launch; it can finish while another design is active, but may only write to its originating document and must not overwrite a newer revision. An Analytic design domain may also act as a transition driver in the same document; both roles reference one authoritative SDF, so a transform invalidates all of their dependents. The first release admits one Design domain source per document; compound field Boolean domains remain a separate future composition feature. Analytic design domains may be moved, rotated, or resized through the existing manipulator; these edits immediately update the authoritative SDF and dependent guidance, clear dependent results, and do not silently regenerate them.
